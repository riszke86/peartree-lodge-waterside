Welcome to Peartree Lodge

A Comfortable Stay in a Peaceful Location

Peartree Lodge Waterside offers comfortable and affordable accommodation in Milton Keynes. Our hotel combines a quiet waterside setting with convenient access to the city centre, local businesses and Milton Keynes University Hospital.

Whether you are visiting for work, a family break or a longer stay, our rooms are designed to provide the practical facilities and comfort you need.
